# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners checking municipal tap water by ZIP, then choosing a filter package. Patrick/Ankit use the page as a sellable proof for a water-health offer.

## Product Purpose

Turn a ZIP into a water health assessment and a retail filter quote. Success is a report that looks like the Parker v5 PDF, with honest EPA vs EWG labels, and a Buy action.

## Positioning

Live EPA identity plus sourced health-guideline fold-overs (EWG/CA PHG), not an EPA compliance letter dressed as a scare page.

## Operating Context

Public Next.js on Vercel. Search ZIP, pick PWS, read assessment, buy on retailer sites.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live EPA SDWIS/ECHO search.
- Parker `CO0118040` has a sourced EWG 2013-2024 occurrence table.
- Other ZIPs must not invent 13-of-38 counts.
- No in-app payments. Prices are sourced retail.
- No PHI. ZIP is not stored.

- [inferred] Marketing-executive perspective: photography-led, not dashboard blocks.

## Evidence on Hand

- Parker v5 PDF example.

## Product Principles

1. Label EPA vs EWG on every number.
2. Do not invent occurrence counts.
3. The page should sell by showing water in the home, not by stacking boxes.
4. Proof stays checkable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text contrast at least 4.5:1. Images have alt text. Reduced-motion respected.
