# Design

Photography-led water health assessment as a continuous ribbon. Cool stone ground, river-slate ink, rust only in type and hairline bars. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body. Photos abut with no tile gaps. Status is a line of words, not filled rectangles. Sheet measure is about 44rem. EPA vs EWG labels stay in copy.

Images: Unsplash stock in `public/img/` with `PROVENANCE.json`.
