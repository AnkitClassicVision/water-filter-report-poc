# Design

Photography-led water health assessment. Cool stone ground, river-slate ink, rust only for out-of-range proof. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body. Full-bleed tap, glass, shower, source, and kitchen photos. Numbers sit on or beside images. No four-up metric cards. EPA vs EWG labels stay in copy.

Images: Unsplash stock in `public/img/` with `PROVENANCE.json`.
